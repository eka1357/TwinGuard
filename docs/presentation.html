Why Open-Loop Physical AI Fails

Problem vs. Solution

❌ The Problem: Open-Loop Blindness

Current Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models generate motor trajectories without continuous verification:

  • ⚠️ Unmodeled Physics: Grasp slippage, friction variations, or stuck drawers cause immediate failure.
  • ⚠️ Zero Error Recovery: Open-loop controllers continue moving through empty air, dropping dishes and causing catastrophic collisions.
  • ⚠️ Rigid Arm Isolation: Dual-arm setups usually operate as two single arms rather than coordinated bimanual partners.

✅ The TwinGuard Solution: Closed-Loop AI

A hierarchical Physical AI architecture combining multimodal planning with self-healing:

  • 🛡️ Dual-Layer Verifier: SafetyVerifier checks contact normal forces and drop limits; VisualGuard checks visual anomalies.
  • 🔄 Dynamic Self-Healing: Automated re-observation and replanning that turns a failed grasp into a successful recovery.
  • 🤝 True Bimanual Handover: Mid-air object transfer between arms with synchronized clearance.
